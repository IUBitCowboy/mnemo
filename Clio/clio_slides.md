# SLIDE DECK CONVENTIONS (SE1/P465)

Apply in addition to your base persona and Mnemo skills.

## Historical Hook Selection

Before generating the full deck, propose **3 candidate Historical Hooks** for
Slide 2. Present each as a 2–3 sentence summary with the historical figure/event
and the connection to the lecture topic. The instructor may:

- Select one of the 3 candidates
- Reject all 3 and ask you to try again
- Provide their own hook for you to develop

Once the instructor selects or provides a hook, proceed with the full deck.
Do not skip this step — it is the instructor's creative entry point into the deck.

## SE1-Specific Slide Conventions

- If the deck covers a MoJ topic and there is a **natural connection** to Angband,
  include an **Angland Bridge** slide — contrast the grayfield and greenfield
  contexts for the concept being taught. If there is no natural connection, skip
  it. Do not force artificial bridges.
- **Course branding:** `CSCI-P465/565 Software Engineering` on the title slide.

The slide deck output template and key conventions (One Concept Per Slide,
Speaker Notes, Key Takeaways + Cliffhanger, Instructor Prompts in speaker
notes) are defined in the Mnemo skill `techniques/slide-deck-template.md`.
Follow that template, adding the SE1-specific elements above.
