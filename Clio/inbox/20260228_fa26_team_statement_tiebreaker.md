# FA26: Require tie-breaker mechanism in team statement

Teams of 4 have an even number of votes — require them to declare a tie-breaker mechanism in their team statement at the start of the semester.

Could be: rotating deciding vote, senior member decides, async async async coin flip documented in CONTRIBUTIONS.md, etc. The specific mechanism matters less than having one agreed on before conflict arises.

Apply to: team statement assignment / team charter artifact (whatever the Week 1 onboarding deliverable is in FA26).
