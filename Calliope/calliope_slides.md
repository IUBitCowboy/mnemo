# SLIDE DECK CONVENTIONS (C291)

Apply in addition to your base persona and Mnemo skills.

## Slide Deck Structure for C291

C291 uses a slide sequence that embeds the in-class exercise directly
in the deck:

1. **Title** — lecture title and week
2. **Learning Objectives** — what students will be able to do after
   this lecture
3. **Conceptual Theory** — the core concept, with analogies only where
   they serve a pedagogical purpose
4. **Worked Example** — a complete, annotated code example
5. **In-Class Exercise** — the ICEX (15–30 min). For standard ICEX,
   this is "The Bug in the Path" format (below). For gate ICEX,
   ceremonies, and audit labs, use the appropriate format from
   `calliope_icex.md`.
6. **AI Prompts** — suggested prompts for students to use with AI tools
   (varies by phase — see below)
7. **Key Takeaways** — summary of what was learned

## Key Elements

### "The Bug in the Path"

The standard C291 ICEX, embedded in the slide deck. Appears after the
worked example and before the AI Prompts slide. Includes the
Theory/Experiment table from `calliope_icex.md`. Not all sessions use
this format — gate ICEX, ceremonies, and audit labs have their own
formats defined in `calliope_icex.md`.

### Cliffhanger

Optional. When used, appears on the last lecture of the week only — not
every session. An unsolved problem or question that builds anticipation
for the next session. Do not force a cliffhanger where the material
doesn't naturally produce one.

### AI Prompts Slide

The AI Prompts slide evolves across the semester:

**Author phase (W1–10):** Suggested prompts for students to use with AI
tools to visualize concepts before writing code (memory layouts, pointer
arithmetic, stack frames). AI for *visualization before implementation*,
not AI for implementation itself.

**Gate ceremonies:** After the ceremony, the AI Prompts slide shows the
two agent-generated solutions alongside the student's, with the critique
worksheet. The "prompt" is the specification that produced all three
versions.

**Auditor phase (W11–15):** The AI Prompts slide becomes "Agent Prompt +
Predicted Output + Actual Output + Discrepancy Analysis." Students
predict what an agent will produce before running it, then compare.
This is the predict-before-run protocol from the base persona.

### Narrative Tone

Professional and technical. No epic journey metaphor, "Hero's
Homecoming" framing, or Kernel-as-High-King analogies unless they
directly help students understand the material. Quality is the
governing principle. C291's slide 1 is Title, slide 2 is Learning
Objectives — no Historical Hook (Clio convention, not Calliope's).

### General Slide Deck Template

The general slide deck output template and conventions (One Concept
Per Slide, Speaker Notes, etc.) are defined in the Mnemo skill
`techniques/slide-deck-template.md`. Follow that template, inserting the
C291-specific elements above.
