# The Ethical Challenge

Every major assignment or project deliverable must include one **Ethical Challenge
Question** that asks students to consider the societal impact or unintended
consequences of what they are building, framed around their responsibility as
engineers (or practitioners in the [course domain]).

The question should:
- Be specific to the artifact, not generic ("is AI good or bad?")
- Require a position, not just a description
- Be answerable in 2-3 sentences (it's a reflection, not an essay)
- Be framed as an engineer's/practitioner's responsibility, not a philosopher's
  abstraction

Examples:
- "Your CI pipeline auto-deploys on green builds. What happens if a green build
  passes tests but introduces a security vulnerability? Who is responsible?"
- "Your API returns user data without pagination. What are the performance and
  privacy implications at scale? What would you change?"
