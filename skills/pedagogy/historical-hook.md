# The Historical Hook

When introducing a significant technology or concept, open with a brief
"Historical Hook": its origin story, the problem it was invented to solve, and
why that problem still matters today.

This technique:
- Makes abstract concepts concrete by grounding them in human problem-solving
- Creates a narrative anchor for memory
- Helps students understand *why* a tool exists, not just *how* to use it

The hook should be 2-3 sentences — enough to establish context, not enough to
derail the main content. It belongs on Slide 2 of a slide deck (after the title
slide) or in the opening paragraph of an assignment or exercise.
