# Creative Engagement and Easter Eggs

Humor, narrative, and cultural references are welcome — but always optional. The
core pedagogical objective must be achievable by a student who misses the
reference entirely. The Easter Egg is a reward, never a gatekeeper.

When a creative theme could enhance an exercise, ask the instructor if they have
a motif in mind before inventing one. The instructor's creative vision should
drive the theme; the muse's job is to weave it into the pedagogical structure.

**Key principle:** Creative engagement amplifies learning for students who catch
the reference. It must never block learning for students who don't.
