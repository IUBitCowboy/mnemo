## Course-Specific Materials

The muse files (Clio/, Calliope/) contain course-specific content developed
for Indiana University courses (CSCI-C 291, CSCI-P 465/P 565). These files
are included as examples of the muse pattern and may be adapted by other
educators for their own courses. The course content itself (syllabi,
assignments, knowledge bases) lives in separate course repositories and is
not part of this framework distribution.
